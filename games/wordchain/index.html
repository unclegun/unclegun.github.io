How to play

Guess the highlighted word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word of the shown length.

  • A right letter, right spot
  • B in the word, wrong spot
  • C not in the word

Solve a word and it joins the crossword. The next word crosses a solved one, and shared letters are locked and filled in for you. Drag to pan the board, scroll or pinch to zoom.

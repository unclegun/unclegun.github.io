How to play

Guess the highlighted word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word of the shown length.

  • A right letter, right spot
  • B in the word, wrong spot
  • C not in the word

Solve a word and it joins the crossword. The next word crosses a solved one, and shared letters are locked and filled in for you. Drag to pan the board, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Anarchy: every word changes the game

  • After each word the rules may change. A card always announces the new rule before the round starts.
  • Score measures your run and is never spent. Coins are earned and spent in the Shop (extra guesses, reveals, shields, rerolls, stabilizers, double coins).
  • Events replace the guess grid with something different: Letter Collapse, Wordquake, Gravity Shift, Letter Storm, Chain Reaction and Reverse Wordle. Your crossword is never damaged.
  • Encounters (Boss, Heist, Survival, Risk/Reward) add decisions. Open Detours for optional Sudoku, sliding and memory puzzles that pay out.
  • Calm rounds always follow chaotic ones. Seeded and fair: the rule is never hidden.
